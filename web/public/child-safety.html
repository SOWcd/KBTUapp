KBTU App: Child safety standards

KBTU App is an unofficial app for students of Kazakh-British Technical University. It has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

What is not allowed

Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors, including CSAM, grooming, sextortion and trafficking. Accounts that do this are removed at once and the content is deleted.

How to report

Every post, comment, profile and event in the app has a Report button. Reports go to our moderators, who act on CSAE reports first. You can also write to fanistries@gmail.com.

What we do

We remove the content and the account, keep what the law requires, and report confirmed CSAM to the relevant authorities of the Republic of Kazakhstan and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

Law

We comply with the child safety laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the laws of the countries where the app is offered.

Contact
