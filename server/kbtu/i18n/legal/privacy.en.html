Privacy Policy

Edition dated 12 November 2026

KBTU App is a student app in Telegram. This policy explains what data it needs, why, who can see it and how to delete it. The policy is drawn up under the Republic of Kazakhstan Law No. 94-V of 21 May 2013, On Personal Data and Their Protection. The Russian text prevails over translations.

KBTU App is not an official service of Kazakh-British Technical University JSC and does not act on its behalf.

1. The operator and your consent

The owner and operator of the personal data database is the creator of KBTU App, Nurmatov Sukhrab Rustamzhanovich. For questions about data, corrections, access and deletion, contact @Sowers on Telegram.

When you sign in, you give general consent to the collection, storage, use, modification, transfer, publication, de-identification and deletion described here. It covers only the specified categories, purposes and recipients, including transfers abroad in section 9. We keep the consent version and time. Consent lasts as long as the data is needed for these purposes, subject to the periods in section 10 and your right to withdraw it.

This policy describes both available features and features already planned: document search, support awards, shared classes and activity points. Describing them does not mean they are already available. Enabling a described feature does not in itself require new general consent. Optional data appears when you use the relevant feature or when a club provides its list as described below.

Event reports, appeals to Student Government and the volunteers ledger still have a separate choice within those features. AI help with an appeal is also optional. Declining them should not block the rest of the app. See sections 3, 4 and 6.

Guest mode is available without general consent. It also processes data: on the first opening, before sign-in and consent, the app creates a record with Telegram data and the first and last opening times.

2. Sign-in, verification and profile data

This data is needed to link your account to you, verify your student status, fill in your profile and contact you.

  • Telegram: ID, first and last name, @username, profile photo link, language and permission for the bot to message you. A copy of the photo is stored on our server for display in the app.
  • Microsoft KBTU: account identifier, first and last name, @kbtu.kz login and email address, account type, creation date and country of use. You enter your password only on the Microsoft page; we do not see it. If you then leave your student card number and year of study incomplete, the information waits for 7 days and is deleted.
  • What you enter: last name, first name, student card number and year of study; in settings, school, phone number and city. Until Microsoft verification, sign-in details are marked unverified. You can hide, change or delete your city, and change or delete your phone number.
  • Former portal sign-in: until 19 October 2026, it supplied full name, student ID, year of study, school and programme, university email, phone number and timetable. That sign-in method is closed. Information already received remains until account deletion. We never stored your portal password.
  • Club lists: KBTU's Department of Youth Policy provided the Student Union club lists for autumn 2026: club, position, department, full name, school, year of study, city, Telegram username or university email, and phone number if given. A row is not used for membership until it is linked to your KBTU account by username or by email and name. Once linked, it becomes a membership record, and the city and phone number fill in your profile. You can leave the club.
  • Lists from organisers of KBTU-related events: full name, university email, student card number, school, year of study, phone number and participation. On Microsoft verification, information is linked by the same email or by student card number and name, fills in your profile and grants participation awards. We do not then disclose it to third parties. Before sign-in, only the expected-participant lists described in section 3 are used. Information linked to you is deleted when you delete your account.

You can add a nickname, photo, bio and award showcase to your profile. A nickname does not hide your name in your profile. We do not request your KBTU password, access to your account, grades or GPA, transcript, individual identification number or portal photo. Do not add these to free text or photos: content you send yourself is processed under the sections below.

3. Studies, connections and participation

This data is needed for the timetable, finding people you know, event registration, entry, club operations and participation records.

  • Classes: subject, its code if any, day of the week, time, room, teacher and names in different languages. You can send the bot a timetable page from the portal. It extracts your name, student card number and classes; the file itself is not stored. If the name is yours but the number differs while having the same admission year and mode of study, the number is corrected automatically. This uses your single number correction; the old and new values go into the log.
  • Shared classes: matching by class code, day of the week, time and room is planned. A student will see who else in the app attends that class with them. Display will be on by default and can be turned off in settings. Other people's full timetables will not be disclosed.
  • Connections: friends, subscriptions, saved events, invitations and who invited whom. Opening someone's link before sign-in leaves a record of the inviter. After sign-in, the invitation is credited to them: they see how many people joined and their Inviter award, but not those people's names.
  • Events and entry: registration, waiting list, ticket, verification QR code, check-in time and, where available, check-out time. A guest without an account can be registered for a free event with their full name and arrival time. After Microsoft verification, a check-in from the last 30 days is linked by name only if no other student has the same name. Unlinked check-ins older than 30 days are deleted when the next guest is registered.
  • Expected participants: a QR check-in organiser can upload full names, emails, student card numbers, schools, years of study, phone numbers, Telegram details and other fields from their list, including people without accounts. Before those people sign in, the list is already used for the attendance and absence table. When someone signs in with Microsoft using the same email, their student card number is filled in from it. The table with all fields is available to the organisers of that check-in. See section 10 for retention periods.
  • Applications and feedback: answers to event registration and club application questions, a volunteer application with a note, event rating and review, a meeting invitation response and a reason for declining. Club recruitment stores selected departments, answers, reviewers' scores and notes, interview time and attendance.
  • Event reports: for internal club records, each team member's mark per event (present, late, absent or excused), who first filled in the report and who last edited it, with the times of those actions; the club's department that ran the event, its own guest count and its free-text note that may mention people.
  • Membership: club, role, position, departments, tags, joining date, probation period and the history of joining, role changes and leaving. Leaders use the indicator of 60 days without club events and the number of guests you invited to manage the team. Role history is recorded automatically.
  • Skills and finding people: selected skills (up to five), added skill names, Open to projects status, notifications; a request's type, title, text, skills, expiry, status and whether you found someone; offers to help or connect, their senders, recipients and decisions; blocks, reports and records of notifications already sent. This data is not collected until you open this feature. Skill search and suggesting you to others work only with Open to projects enabled; it is off by default. App staff, the Department of Youth Policy and guests are not suggested. People who have blocked each other cannot see each other here.

For a co-hosted event, the host club and the co-hosting club that accepted participation each keep a separate report about their own team. A person who belongs to both clubs can be marked in each report. Each report can be read and changed only by people from the club it belongs to: its president, vice-presidents and members whose role the club has given the event reports right. Other members, clubs and app staff do not get access through the interface. The app does not send report content to the bot, AI services or other third parties; the general rules for secure connections and encrypted backups are in sections 9 and 10.

Before you first open or fill in a report, the app asks for separate consent and stores its version and time. Each team member gives or withdraws this consent themselves using the Mark me in reports switch on their club page. There is one consent per person for reports of every club they belong to. Without it, the server refuses to mark that person. The club must explain in advance to each team member what marks are kept about them, who sees them and how long they are kept. The report writer's consent does not replace anyone else's.

Membership can come from a Telegram chat. Only the app's creator links a main chat to a club and department chats to departments. The bot is an administrator without additional permissions. It receives Telegram IDs and membership statuses for joining, leaving or removal and asks Telegram whether a student is in the chat. Chat messages are not read or stored for synchronisation. Data about people without accounts is not stored. Only students verified through Microsoft with valid consent are included; blocked students and those who requested deletion are excluded.

Being in the main chat adds you to the club as a regular member; leaving or removal removes you from the club and its departments. A department chat changes only the department of someone already in the club. The president is not removed automatically. After verification and consent, earlier chat memberships are also checked; current membership is reconciled nightly. After manual removal, synchronisation does not return you until you rejoin the relevant chat. Rejoining does not lift a club block.

If the bot loses administrator status, Telegram refuses a check or reconciliation would remove more than three people and more than half the membership, that chat is paused. Pending changes are cancelled. Its membership does not change until the creator restarts it.

4. Payments, awards, hours and points

This data is needed to verify payment to the organiser and record participation and support.

  • Paid ticket: Kaspi receipt photo, its link to the ticket and verification result. The receipt is recognised on our server without an external service. We store the amount, receipt number, sender's and recipient's names, payment time and indicators of possible errors. The organiser receives the money; the app does not get access to the bank account.
  • Awards: which award was given, to whom, when and on what basis. A Fuel award for voluntary support through Telegram Stars, recording the giver and amount, and a manually granted Club Founder award are planned. Fuel is only a thank-you, with no goods or services in return. Only the app's creator will be able to see the giver and amount. Public display of the award itself is described in section 8.
  • Volunteer hours: the app keeps a ledger on the club's instructions. The club supplied the list and determines the purpose; the app stores it and counts hours. It may include people without accounts. All supplied columns are stored: for example, full name in Latin script, year of study, school, department, phone number, Telegram, corporate email and monthly columns from the hours table. We store the source and receipt time, opening balance, shifts with check-in, check-out and the person recording them, missed team-building events and the president's corrections: old and new numbers or times, reason, who made the correction and when.
  • Counting hours: from QR check-in and check-out at club events or shifts entered by the president. Without check-out, a shift counts as 0 minutes, but attendance remains. Team-building events do not earn hours. An absence counts only for a completed team-building event where at least a quarter of expected participants, and no fewer than three people, checked in. Monthly columns in an imported table are kept as supplied; they are not automatically calculated hours.
  • Activity points: earning points for participation and the ability to redeem them for rewards are planned. This will involve account-linked records of earning and using points. It is a separate record, not a money balance. Detailed rules and data information will be shown before this feature is used.

The ledger is visible to the club president and members the president authorises. A volunteer with an account sees only their own hours, shifts and absences. Other students, clubs and app staff do not get it through the interface. Only the president can correct hours, absences and check-ins. Consent to ledger processing is requested from those concerned. The club is responsible for having each person's consent to transfer their data, including people without accounts; the president's consent cannot replace theirs. The app's creator may ask the club to confirm that it has these consents. You can have your record deleted through the president or operator, including without an account.

5. Your texts, photos and publications

This data is needed to display what you chose to publish, allow discussion and handle reports. We store the content, the author where applicable, time, publication location, status and review results.

Gallery and albums. A Gallery photo is published under your name or nickname with a profile link and time. You can attach it to an event where you checked in; the event team can add photos under the album rules without that check-in. Event photos are visible to those with access to its album. The main feed respects event access. Reporting a photo hides it for you; reports by three different people hide it for everyone pending a moderator's decision, unless a moderator has already kept it. There is an I am in the photo and object reason. You can delete your publication; deleting your account deletes Gallery publications, including those attached to events.

Public forum channel. The bot publishes posts sent from the app in @KBTUappForum. Anyone on the internet can read, forward and find them. Copies, screenshots and search engine caches may remain after deletion and are outside our control. Students verified through Microsoft can post, up to two posts per 10 minutes. Links, @mentions and phone numbers are prohibited in the text.

A named channel post is signed with your first name and last initial; the app shows your nickname, if set, and photo. An anonymous post is signed with a random pseudonym unrelated to your data; early posts use Anonymous. Students, moderators and regular administrators cannot see the author. The app keeps the author link until it is deleted under section 11.

You can add up to four photos to a post. They are re-encoded on our server: EXIF, geotags and other original file metadata are not retained. The file is stored under a random name and sent to the public channel with the post. This does not hide faces, writing or other identifying details in the image itself. A moderator always reviews an anonymous photo post first. AI checks the text, not the image. Two open reports by different people take down a photo post pending a moderator's decision, unless a moderator has already kept it.

Before publication, the AI services in section 9 check the post's text. Only an approved post is published immediately; otherwise, and when anonymous text identifies a particular person, a moderator decides. If AI rejects it, it gives you and the moderator a short explanation; the text is sent to the same services again for this, and the explanation is stored. You can send a post to a moderator up to three times a day. Text, anonymity, status, time and channel message numbers are stored.

Anonymous replies. Through the app, an anonymous post's author can reply in the discussion or to a specific comment using its link. The bot publishes only text with the 🎭 mask and the post's pseudonym; early posts use 🎭 Post author. Links and @mentions are prohibited. AI checks the text; insults, bullying, other people's data or references to a third person require a moderator's decision. The review explanation is stored in the same way as for a post. Replies must be at least 10 seconds apart. The daily limit under a post is the number of other students' comments the bot has seen, plus five.

A reply stores its text, the number of the comment it replies to without that comment's text, status, time and bot message number. There is no separate link between a reply and an account: only through the post. Only you see it as yours. You can delete a reply; a moderator can take it down. Replies are removed with the post. A reply can be reported using /report; the reporter and time are stored for as long as the reply or the reporter's account exists. Three reports by students whose verification is long-standing take down the reply pending a moderator's decision. Notifications of someone else's reply use a template without their text. Your style, timing and comments from your own Telegram account may identify you.

Limits of anonymity. Only the app's creator can identify the author of an anonymous post, a reply through its post, or an appeal to Student Government. This is permitted only in an exceptional case: threats, a violation of law or a lawful request from a public authority. Every lookup is recorded in the action log. Regular moderators and administrators cannot do this. The text or photos themselves may also identify the author.

Comments and reactions. The app stores your comments and reactions under events and posts. Channel comments are written in the linked Telegram group: anyone who opens the discussion sees the text and your Telegram name and photo, rather than your app profile. The bot checks the commenter's Telegram ID: only verified students can comment. We do not copy ordinary comment text except for reports and the cases in section 7. For the feed, we record the post, time, comment number and account; app photos of up to ten recent commenters are shown under a post without names. Comments deleted after reports and blocked accounts are not shown there. Telegram reactions arrive only as totals: we do not know or store who made them.

Clubs' public materials. If the team links its main public Telegram channel, its posts are copied to club news: text, photos, views and reaction counts. Videos and files remain in Telegram and open from the post; channel comments are not copied. Deleting a channel post also takes it down here and erases its text and photos. Turning copying off does not delete material already copied. For deletion, contact @Sowers. When suggesting a chat for the directory through the app or bot, you supply links: the rest of the message text is not stored, and the person who suggested it is not shown anywhere.

6. Private communication with the service and appeals

This data is needed to answer your question, send an appeal to the recipient you choose or fix an error.

Assistant and document search. AI receives your question and several previous messages, school and year of study, today's and tomorrow's classes (subject, time, room), upcoming public events and campus places. A question from a club or event page also includes that page's publicly available content. Your name, student ID, email and phone number from your profile are not added. Document search is planned: to find relevant documents, the question text will also be sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to calculate its numerical representation, an embedding. No profile is attached to that request. The text itself may contain personal data: it is not removed.

Assistant questions and answers are not stored on the server, except what you separately submit as feedback. To enforce the limit of 30 requests per day, each request time is stored for 2 days. This limit also includes an announcement an organiser sends to AI to fill in an event: we do not store the announcement itself.

To improve the assistant, we store, without an account or name: a short topic of a question it could not answer, written by AI in English without names, and 👍 / 👎 ratings with the topic and day, without text. After 👎, you can separately press Send: administrators then see one question and answer without a name or account. Your subjects, times and rooms are removed from the answer; names and contacts within the text remain. These reviews and topics are deleted after 30 days. If there are signs of severe distress, helpline numbers are shown. We do not record to whom or why they were shown, only a weekly total. AI can make mistakes.

Appeals to Student Government. The app asks for separate consent before your first submission; if you request AI help earlier, before that help. The consent version and time are stored. Your draft stays on your device and is not stored on the server. You choose a named or anonymous appeal and an optional topic. We store the text, topic, anonymity, status, reply, submission time, response deadline, first reply time and closure time. The account link is stored separately for your list, deletion and notifications; submission times for the daily limit are kept for one day.

Only you and Student Government members given special access by the creator can read, reply and change status. A club role, moderator role or administrator role does not itself grant access. For a named appeal they see only your first name and last initial, without ID, profile or contacts. For an anonymous appeal, they see none of these. Views, status changes, replies and access grants and revocations are logged without text. The author identification rule is in section 5.

AI is used only when you press Help. Before a draft is first sent to AI, you must separately choose to allow transfer abroad; consent to submit an appeal does not itself include this. The text goes to the services in section 9 without your profile, but with any data in the text. AI suggests what to clarify, does not rewrite the appeal and does not decide for you whether to send it. The text and result used for suggestions are not stored; the request time is stored for 2 days. Submitting an appeal does not require AI help. At the first reply, the bot sends a template with a button, without the appeal or reply text, if you have allowed it to message you.

Student Government and the creator see monthly counts of stored appeals by topic: every appeal is counted, without matching authors. A topic with fewer than five appeals in a month is grouped into Other. This is not a count of unique people. You can delete an appeal at any time; automatic deletion periods are in section 10. Readers may make copies or screenshots; circumstances and style may identify an anonymous author. The queue is not an emergency service.

Map. An error note (up to 300 characters), selected place and account go to the operator. They are stored until the error is fixed and for 30 days after that. After account deletion, the note remains without your name.

Notifications and contacts. The bot sends templates with a button into the app; you can turn notifications off in settings. Exceptions are your channel posts and anonymous replies, your restored comment that the bot returns only to you, and the title of your own people-finding request in a message to you. With people-finding notifications enabled, the bot messages you about suitable projects at most once a day and twice a week, with a button to turn them off. Another person's Telegram contact is disclosed in people search only after both agree: one makes an offer and the other accepts in the bot.

7. Reports, protection against abuse and statistics

Reports contain the reported item, reason, your note if any, reporter's account, time and decision. Moderators need them to check a violation, hide material, take it down or restore it. The author is not told who reported it.

People-finding requests and new skill names are checked by AI: only the text, without a name or ID. If checking is unavailable, the request waits for a moderator, usually up to 24 hours. After three reports from accounts older than two weeks, a request is hidden only with AI or moderator confirmation; the author is told the reason.

In a Telegram discussion, /report under a comment creates a report. The command is deleted and confirmation is sent privately if the bot can message you. We store the comment text at the time of reporting and after edits, the author's Telegram ID and account, link, reporters and decision. AI checks only the text. Two reports by students with accounts older than two weeks, together with a violation found by AI, lead to deletion; a moderator confirms or reverses it. Otherwise, a moderator decides. If reversed, the author receives their own text in a private message if the bot can message them.

The bot immediately deletes an unverified person's comment. Its text, discussion link, Telegram ID and time are stored for one hour so the text can be returned only to that person when they open the app with the same signed Telegram account. The warning disappears from the discussion after a minute. The Telegram ID and warning time are kept for 90 days; another comment leads to a group block. KBTU verification lifts it. Name and @username are not stored for this record.

For Telegram reports, their number and confirmed decisions are counted. Students do not see these numbers. The creator may receive a suggestion to invite an accurate reporter to become a moderator; a person decides. Two instances of report abuse recognised by a moderator within 30 days block reporting until an administrator's decision.

Request logs exist for protection and service operation: time, address and errors. Views of profiles outside your friends and shared clubs are tracked to prevent bulk copying: up to 200 per day, with records kept for 2 days. Administrators can see who opened the app on a selected day: name or nickname, @username and KBTU verification. These daily records are kept for 30 days. Staff actions and previous values of corrected data go into a log. We store which staff notifications each staff member turned off.

Separately, we count totals of screen openings, actions, errors, referral sources, opening speed and usage duration, including steps in the appeal form. These are daily sums without text, user ID, Telegram ID, IP, person hash or session on the row. They are kept for 180 days. These counters do not replace the account-based visit records described above.

8. Who can access what

  • All students: first name and last initial, nickname (instead of your name in the feed and lists, alongside it in your profile), photo, card number, year of study, school, bio, awards without dates, number of events attended and city unless hidden. Photos and posts are visible within section 5's limits; shared classes only within section 3's scope.
  • Friends and people in your club: also clubs, former organisations with dates, event badges and award dates. Friends see where you are going if Friends can see where I am going is enabled; it is on by default. Moderators and administrators see the same in your profile except upcoming events.
  • Club page visitors: membership with first name and last initial or nickname, photo, role and position. Only members and app moderators and administrators can see a hidden membership list. In Message the club, everyone sees the president's Telegram username, or the vice-president's if the president has none. It is changed in Telegram. The creator's and chief administrator's names, photos and usernames are visible in Support and ideas.
  • Organisers of your event or club: full name, @username, student ID, email and phone number when you register for an event or apply. Organisers see their event's ticket, receipt and question answers. They can receive participant and expected-participant tables as files in Telegram; the copies remain with them. They see a volunteer application with its note, time, decision, full name, year of study, school and @username. Club leaders see ratings and reviews without names and only once there are at least three ratings.
  • Entry helper: until the event ends, the name and photo of the person being checked in. If entrusted with the whole entry process, also the registration list with first names and last initials. The president, vice-president or administrator invites the helper, including by QR. The check-in screen shows confirmation; the team device shows the checked-in person's name.
  • Your club team: full name, @username, role, position, departments, tags and joining date. Leaders also see probation period, the indicator of 60 days without events, the number of invited guests and the reason for declining a meeting. The president, vice-president and those authorised to manage the team can export membership to Telegram: full name, @username, year of study, school, role, position, department and joining date.
  • Recruitment team: profile, email, @username, answers and selected departments; club events attended, number of friends in the club, previous applications and account creation date.
  • KBTU's Department of Youth Policy: for club records, full name, student ID, year of study, school, role, joining date, last club event and the indicator of 60 days without events. For presidents and vice-presidents, also @username, university email and phone number for contact. The register may be exported to Telegram. When preparing a letter from a club, your name, position and phone number are filled in; you send or copy the letter yourself.
  • Special recipients: only the people in section 3 see event reports, those in section 4 see the hours ledger, and those in section 6 see appeals. For planned points redemption, recipients and the scope of data they need will be stated before use of the feature begins. Student Government membership alone does not open the whole database.
  • Calendar service and link holder: connecting a calendar creates a secret link to your events. It can be read without signing in. You can reset the link in Settings → Calendar.
  • Only you: subscriptions, saved items and your full timetable. Limited timetable use for shared classes and the assistant is described above.

For serious violations, the app's creator personally passes the material and only the information needed for an investigation to the university's disciplinary bodies, only where there is a lawful basis. This does not automatically open accounts, anonymous authors or private appeals to them. The rules on revealing anonymous authors still apply.

We do not sell data or share it with advertisers. Consent for your own data does not entitle you to transfer or publish other people's data without a basis.

10. Storage and retention periods

The database is on a server in Almaty, Kazakhstan. Every night a backup is encrypted on our server and stored in Cloudflare R2 for about 30 days. Only the operator can decrypt it: the key is neither on the server nor with Cloudflare. Our server also has unencrypted copies: the last 14 nightly copies, no older than 15 days, and copies made before database updates from the last 14 days.

Unless another period is given below, account data is kept while the account exists. The volunteers ledger has its own period: while the club maintains it, with no automatic deletion; you can request deletion of your record. Club and department membership is kept until you leave, and history and related logs while the account exists.

  • Incomplete Microsoft sign-in: 7 days. Unlinked guest names: 30 days, deleted when the next guest is registered. Expected-participant lists: 30 days after the check-in session ends, or 90 days from creation for an unfinished session.
  • Reports and team marks are kept as internal club records, including after a person leaves the club, without an automatic period in days. When the host removes the co-hosting club or that club leaves, that club's own report and all its attendance marks for the event are deleted immediately. A mark is deleted when the person withdraws consent to reports, their account or the event is deleted, the co-hosting participation of the club that owns the report ends, or someone with the reports right clears it. The report itself is also deleted with its event; it cannot be deleted separately in the app. Links to the person who filled in or last edited a report are removed when they withdraw consent or their account is deleted; the note stays with the report.
  • Event question answers: 30 days after the event. Ordinary club application answers: 60 days after the decision. Recruitment answers, scores and notes: 60 days after results are sent.
  • Visit days: 30 days; profile views for protection: 2 days. The text of a sent message in our bot queue is erased after 30 days; an unsent message after it is sent and that period has elapsed.
  • A people-finding request with offers: 30 days after expiry; its notification records: 30 days. Skills and settings: until account deletion; an added skill name remains in the shared list without a name.
  • AI request times: 2 days; assistant topics, ratings and submitted reviews: 30 days. A map note: until correction and another 30 days. Aggregate usage counters: 180 days.
  • An appeal, reply and account link: 90 days after closure, but no more than 180 days after submission. Submission records for the daily limit: one day.
  • Copies of reported Telegram comments and the reports themselves: 30 days. If the bot has not yet been able to delete a comment from Telegram, its copy remains until successful deletion. Text for an unverified author: 1 hour; their warning and block: 90 days. A ban on reporting: until lifted or account deletion.
  • A post deleted by a club loses its text, photos, translations and comments immediately. A post taken down by a moderator loses them 30 days after takedown; the fact it existed and its reaction count remain. A deleted app comment is hidden, but its text is kept until its author's account, the event or the post is deleted.
  • A channel post deleted by its author loses text and photos immediately; an empty record for the limit remains for one day (after a request to a moderator, no earlier than one day after that request). A channel post taken down is erased 30 days after it was written. A selected but unattached photo is deleted in the next cleanup after one hour from upload. Files are deleted as the bot queue is processed. Published post photos are kept with the post.
  • A deleted anonymous reply loses its text immediately; an empty record for limits remains for 30 days from writing. A reply taken down or not yet reviewed: 30 days from writing, once it is no longer in the group.
  • A Gallery or album photo taken down by a moderator is kept for 30 days to allow the decision to be reversed.
  • A chat's club link and reconciliation state: until unlinking. Pending additions and removals with account and chat: until reconciliation ends; they are erased on a pause, restart or unlinking. The queue after verification or consent keeps the account and time until processing. These queues have no fixed period in days.

Profile, album, cover and post photos open by direct link without sign-in. Browsers and intermediary services may keep copies for up to a year; deletion on our side does not erase them. Copies of Telegram publications and recipients' exports are also outside our control.

After actual account deletion, data subject to deletion leaves the live database and leaves backups within 30 days. For ordinary deletion after the one-week cancellation period, this is no later than 37 days from the request. If deletion is postponed because of a future paid event, the backup period starts at actual deletion. To prevent data returning after restoration, the deletion day, a Telegram ID hash with a secret salt and your photo deletion choice are stored separately. This record is deleted after 40 days.

11. Access, correction, deletion and withdrawal

You can view data in your profile and request the full list from @Sowers. You can also request correction, blocking or destruction of data there if it is processed unlawfully. You can contact the authorised personal data protection authority or a court.

You can change your nickname, photo and bio in your profile, and phone number and city in settings. Your student card number and each full-name field in Cyrillic and Latin script can be corrected once; filling an empty Latin-script field does not use a correction. The number may have a different admission year, but not first-year undergraduate status unless that is your status. Your first and last names must be those of your KBTU account in another spelling. Your year of study can be changed once and cannot be lower than the year indicated by the number. You can change your school once and return to the previous one once; filling an empty school does not use a correction, and an entered school cannot be removed. Old values remain in the log until account deletion. Your email is your Microsoft KBTU login and cannot be changed. For anything else, contact the operator.

Account deletion: Settings → Delete account. Deletion takes place after 7 days; you can cancel before then. With a paid ticket to a future event, deletion waits until the event ends. If you are a president, the club passes to the next member by seniority, or to an available administrator if there are no other members. If there is no one to take over, appointment of a new president waits for an administrator.

  • Deleted: student and Microsoft profile, timetable, app invitation, tickets and receipts, reactions, friends, subscriptions, saved items, awards, card number (never reassigned), membership and role history, recruitment answers, personal photo, nickname, bio, city and phone number; Telegram name, photo and username are erased. People-finding data, appeals with replies and links, Telegram comment copies and reports, and commenter records under posts are deleted. Volunteers ledger records linked to you, including previously unlinked records, are deleted with shifts and absences, as are both chat synchronisation queues.
  • Club reports: your team marks and links to you as the person who filled in or last edited a report are deleted. The report itself, its note and action times remain; names and other data within the note are not automatically removed. Contact the operator for access to, correction or deletion of such data.
  • Gallery: all your publications are deleted, including those in event albums. For other photos added to albums using the old method, you can choose Delete my photos too; without that choice they remain without a name. If you appear in someone else's photo and object, report it or contact the operator.
  • Remains without a name or account link: attendance at past events, ratings without reviews, volunteer participation and participation in completed recruitment without answers, scores of others' applications without notes, and event invitations to preserve counts; posts and comments on behalf of a club. Undeleted app comments and published channel posts remain signed Deleted account. For a named post, the bot changes the channel signature; an anonymous post keeps its pseudonym, loses its author link and can no longer be traced to the author even by the creator. Photos in these posts and published anonymous replies stay with the posts. To remove them, delete the post or reply before deleting your account. Unpublished posts and their photos are deleted.
  • Remains linked to your Telegram account: settings, club and app blocks with reasons. Synchronisation logs lose their link to you. To prevent reimporting a deleted volunteer record, import keys are kept only as hashes with a secret key, without the original row.

You can withdraw general consent by deleting your account or contacting the operator. You can withdraw consent to reports using the Mark me in reports switch on a club page without deleting your account: your marks in all reports and links to you as the person who filled in or last edited a report are deleted, while notes remain. Names and other data within notes are not automatically removed. You can resolve a separate issue about report, ledger or appeal data with the operator without deleting your whole account. Withdrawal does not undo actions already lawfully taken or legally required retention. Backup periods and copies outside our control are covered in section 10.

12. Protection and age

The connection is encrypted through HTTPS. Only the operator has database access; app permissions are checked on the server. If a breach occurs, we will notify you through the bot. The app is intended for students aged 16 and over. Where the law requires a legal representative's consent, a student's own tap cannot replace it.

13. Policy changes

We will notify you on opening the app of a minor clarification that adds no data category or processing purpose and does not expand recipients or disclosure. Continuing use after the notice means accepting that change only to the extent permitted by law. The current version is available at this link.

If a new personal data category or purpose is needed, we will first explain it and ask for separate consent within the relevant feature before the first collection or use. We will do the same for expanded transfers or publication not covered by previous consent. Declining leaves that feature unavailable, without requiring all users to consent again. Continuing use and accepting the service terms do not replace that consent.
