Privacy Policy

Version of 23 October 2026. A translation: if it differs from the Russian version, the Russian one applies.

KBTU App is a student app in Telegram for events, clubs and friends. This policy explains which personal data we collect, why, who sees it, where it is kept and how to delete it. It follows the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection" of 21 May 2013 No. 94-V.

KBTU App is not an official service of JSC Kazakh-British Technical University and does not act on its behalf.

1. Who processes the data

The owner and operator of the personal data database is the creator of KBTU App, Sukhrab Rustamzhanovich Nurmatov. For any question about your data: Telegram @Sowers.

2. What we collect

  • From Telegram, when you open the app: your account ID, Telegram first and last name, @username, profile photo link, language.
  • From the KBTU portal, until 19 October 2026, when you signed in with KBTU: last, first and middle name, student ID, year, school and programme, university email, phone, class timetable. We never kept your portal password. That way of signing in is closed; the data already received stays until you delete your account.
  • From your KBTU Microsoft account, when you sign in with Microsoft: the account's ID, first and last name, sign-in name (@kbtu.kz) and the account's mail address, account type (e.g. "student"), the date the account was created and its country of use. The password is typed only on Microsoft's page: we never see or receive it.
  • Typed in by you when you sign in: last name, first name, student ID and year. They are marked unconfirmed until you confirm your KBTU account with Microsoft.
  • What you add: nickname, your own photo, "about me", showcased awards, comments, reactions, club applications, reports.
  • Your activity: event registrations, check-ins, tickets, payment receipt photos (for paid events), saved events, subscriptions, friends, awards.
  • Clubs and role history: which clubs you are in, your role, and when you joined, changed role or left. The history is recorded automatically.
  • Technical data: server logs (request time, address, errors), to run and protect the service; the days you opened the app, for visit statistics only the creator sees; they are kept for 30 days; which profiles you opened of people who are neither your friends nor in your club, so profiles can't be copied in bulk (200 a day at most); kept for 2 days.

We never collect: your KBTU password or account access, grades and GPA, transcript, IIN, portal photos.

3. Why

  • to confirm you are a KBTU student and show your profile;
  • to register you for events, check tickets at the door and keep club rosters;
  • to show your timetable, friends, awards and organisation history;
  • to send notifications through the bot (you can turn them off in settings);
  • to protect the service from abuse and handle reports.

The legal basis is your consent, given when you sign in. Without it the app works in guest mode only.

4. Who sees your data

  • All students: first name and last initial or nickname, photo, card number, year and school, awards (without dates), "about me", number of events attended.
  • Your friends and people in your clubs: also your clubs, past organisations with dates and event badges.
  • Organisers of an event or club, their own only: full name, @username, student ID, email and phone, when you register for their event or apply to their club.
  • The team of a club you are in: full name, @username, role.
  • KBTU's Department of Youth Policy: full name, student ID, year, school and role of club members, to keep the register of student organisations.
  • Only you: subscriptions, saved events, timetable.

We do not sell data or pass it to advertisers.

5. Services the data passes through

  • Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC, UAE): the app opens inside Telegram, the bot sends notifications.
  • Microsoft (Microsoft Corporation, USA): signing in with your KBTU account.
  • Cloudflare (USA): the secure connection between your device and our server; storing the encrypted backups (Cloudflare R2). Also translating the titles and descriptions of public events and the descriptions of clubs into Kazakh, Russian and English (Cloudflare Workers AI): these texts are visible to everyone anyway; Cloudflare doesn't train models on them.
  • The KBTU portal: until 19 October 2026, only at the moment you signed in with KBTU. Not any more.

Telegram, Microsoft and Cloudflare are outside Kazakhstan. By giving consent you also agree to this cross-border transfer.

6. Where and how long

The database is on a server in the Republic of Kazakhstan (Almaty). A backup is made every night, encrypted on our server and kept in Cloudflare R2 for about 30 days; only the operator can decrypt it: the key is neither on the server nor at Cloudflare.

Data is kept while you have an account. The days you opened the app are kept for 30 days; our copies of the bot's messages lose their text after 30 days.

After you delete your account, the data leaves the database at once and the backups within 30 days (no later than 37 days after the request, counting the week to cancel). So that deleted data doesn't come back when a backup is restored, we keep a separate record of the deletion: its time and a hash of the Telegram ID with a secret salt; the record is deleted after 40 days.

7. Your rights

  • See your data in your profile; the full list on request to @Sowers.
  • Correct it: nickname, photo and "about me" in your profile; anything else, write to the operator.
  • Delete your account: in profile settings, "Delete account". It is deleted after 7 days and can be cancelled until then. If you have a paid ticket for an event that hasn't happened yet, the deletion waits until it has.
    Removed: student and Microsoft account details, timetable, tickets and receipts, comments, reactions, friends, subscriptions, saved events, awards, your card number (never given to anyone again), club memberships and role history, answers in club recruitment rounds, your photo, nickname and "about me"; your Telegram name and username are erased.
    Kept without your name and without a link to your account: that you came to past events, event ratings (without comments), volunteering and taking part in finished recruitment rounds (without answers), your votes on others' applications in them (without notes), who invited you to an event, only so the clubs' numbers add up; posts and comments written on behalf of a club; photos you added to event albums (when deleting you can choose "delete my photos too"; a photo showing you is removed on request to @Sowers).
    Kept on your Telegram account: app settings, bans and the number of warnings in clubs (without their text). If you are a club's president, the club passes to the next member by rank, or to the administrator if there is nobody else.
  • Withdraw consent: that is deleting your account.
  • Complain about us to the authorised personal data protection body or a court.

8. Protection

The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Only the operator has access to the database. Club role rights are checked on the server. If a data breach happens, we will tell you through the bot.

9. Age

The app is meant for students aged 16 and over.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will tell you through the bot. The current version is always at this link.
