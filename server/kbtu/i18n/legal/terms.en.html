  • Students run clubs themselves. Club creation is reviewed; official status means registration with KBTU's Department of Youth Policy. When providing a volunteers list, the club is responsible for having each person's consent to transfer their data; the app's creator may ask the club to confirm this.
  • Event reports are internal club records. For a co-hosted event, the host club and the co-hosting club that accepted participation keep separate reports: each about its own team, with its own department, guest count and note. Each report can be read and changed only by the president, vice-presidents and members with the reports right in the club it belongs to; the other club cannot see it. Co-hosting club members also see the co-hosted event's public card in their club's Studio event list; this does not give them access to the other club's report. The club is responsible for accurate marks and explaining the recordkeeping rules to members. Each member gives or withdraws separate consent themselves using the Mark me in reports switch on a club page — one consent for reports of all their clubs; without it, the server refuses to mark them. The report author's consent does not replace anyone else's. Add only information needed for the report to the note, without diagnoses, private-life details or other people's data without a lawful basis.

  • The organiser can enable teams at a free event and set minimum and maximum sizes. When registering, create a named team, join through the captain's link or register without a team for later placement by the app or organiser. Before the event starts, you can leave your team; the captain can rename it, replace its link (the old one stops working) and remove a member, and the organiser can assign participants. Leaving or removal from a team does not cancel event registration. The first member in joining order is captain; when they leave, the next member takes over. The roster is fixed when the event starts. See the policy for who sees the data.

  • An unofficial club's event appears in the main event listings when held jointly with an official club or after reaching the last step of the Path to official status.
