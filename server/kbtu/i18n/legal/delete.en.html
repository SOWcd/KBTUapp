Delete your account

KBTU App: an independent student app in Telegram, the browser, Android and iPhone; not an official service of KBTU

You can delete your KBTU App account and its data yourself, at any time, from any place you use the app: Telegram, the browser, the Android app or the iPhone app. It is the same account everywhere.

In the app

  1. Open KBTU App and go to Profile.
  2. Open Settings and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what goes and what stays, then confirm. The screen shows how long you have to change your mind.

Without the app

If you can't sign in any more, write to @Sowers on Telegram from the account you used, and ask to delete it. We answer within the deadlines the law sets.

What is deleted and what is kept

Your profile, timetable, friends, permissions, receipts and photos are deleted. A few records stay for a limited time, without your name where possible: data needed for a paid ticket that is still due, protection records, and forum posts you didn't delete yourself. The full list and the periods are in the privacy policy, sections 10 and 11.
